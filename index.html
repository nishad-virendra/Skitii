WHAT WE DO

HRV-Adaptive Music Support for Oncology Care

Skitii is an investigational HRV-adaptive music therapy device designed to support anxiety management in oncology settings. It uses heart-rate variability from a wearable sensor to guide adaptive instrumental music during a session and capture relevant session information for review and research.

Explore How Skitii Works Talk to the Team
WHY WE DO IT

Because emotional well-being matters in cancer care.

Cancer treatment can bring significant emotional challenges. Skitii is being developed to explore non-pharmacological support through HRV-adaptive instrumental music.

The human challenge

People undergoing cancer treatment may experience anxiety and emotional distress.

The physiological perspective

Heart-rate variability provides a physiological signal that can be used to guide music adaptation.

The Skitii approach

Investigational technology explores how adaptive instrumental music might support anxiety management.

HOW SKITII WORKS

Sense. Adapt. Record.

Skitii uses heart-rate variability (HRV) from a wearable sensor to guide adaptive instrumental music and record session information for review and research.

01

Sense

Capture HRV using a wearable sensor during a session.

02

Adapt

Use HRV input to guide adaptive instrumental music during the session.

03

Record

Record relevant session information for subsequent review and research.
